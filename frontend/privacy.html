Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 2026

Your privacy matters to us. This page explains what information we collect and how we use it.

1. Information We Collect

When you register, we collect your name, email address, and a securely encrypted password. When you list a property, we collect the property details, photos, and videos you provide. When you make a booking, we record the booking dates and amount paid.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use your information to create and manage your account, process bookings and payments, display property listings, and provide customer support. We do not sell your personal information to third parties.

3. Payment Information

All payments are processed by Paystack, a licensed payment processor. HomeSpace does not receive or store your card details — this is handled entirely and securely by Paystack.

4. Photos and Videos

Property photos and videos you upload are stored securely using Cloudinary, a cloud media hosting service, and are displayed publicly as part of your property listing.

5. Communication

We may contact you regarding your bookings, listings, or account activity. If you contact us via WhatsApp, that conversation is handled through WhatsApp's own platform and is subject to WhatsApp's privacy practices as well.

6. Data Security

We take reasonable steps to protect your information, including encrypting passwords and using secure connections (HTTPS) across the platform. However, no online platform can guarantee complete security, and you should also take care to protect your own account.

7. Your Rights

You may request that we correct or delete your personal information by contacting us directly. Some information (such as records of completed bookings) may need to be retained for legal or accounting purposes.

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this privacy policy as the platform grows. Continued use of HomeSpace after changes means you accept the updated policy.

9. Contact

If you have questions about how your information is handled, please reach out via our WhatsApp Customer Care line.
